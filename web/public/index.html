Method and limitations

  • Scope. Yellow Taxi only, six months (2024-01 to 2024-03 and 2025-01 to 2025-03). Not green taxis, for-hire vehicles or other years, so no claim is made about them.
  • Rules are heuristics. There are no labelled "bad rows", so results are rule violation counts, not precision or recall. Thresholds (100 miles, 80 mph, 24 hours, 6 passengers) are constants in validate.py; changing one changes the numbers.
  • Trusted does not mean correct. A row can pass every rule and still be wrong. "Revenue" is the sum of total_amount, which includes tips, taxes and surcharges, not driver earnings.
  • Error vs warning. Passenger count (R10) and total reconciliation (R12) fire on whole vendors or payment types, so they are reported but do not exclude rows; excluding them would remove most Vendor 1 and flex-fare trips and bias the metrics.
  • Duplicates are counted, not removed. Timestamps are used as published, without time-zone conversion.
  • Verification. The committed aggregates are checked for internal consistency by the test suite and were recomputed separately from the raw Parquet files; each source file's SHA-256 is shown below.
